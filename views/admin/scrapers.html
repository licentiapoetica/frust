Scrapers

Counts what every client fetches: the distinct items it opens and original files it downloads. A client far above what a person does gets a "checking your browser" page that never finishes (in block mode), until its block runs out. Admins and moderators are never counted. Guests are counted per IP (kept only as a keyed hash), signed-in users per account, Tor visitors per circuit.
